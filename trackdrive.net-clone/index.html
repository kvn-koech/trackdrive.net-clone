      How it decides

      Signal. Score. Route.

      Every call goes through the same three steps before a buyer’s phone rings.

      01

      Signal

      Everything known the moment the call connects.

      • Caller ID and line type
      • Source, campaign and keyword
      • Geo, time and day
      • The caller’s first words
      02

      Score

      The call is screened and scored before anyone is dialed.

      • Intent and sentiment
      • Spam and duplicate checks
      • TCPA, DNC and VOIP screening
      • State rules and consent
      03

      Route

      The best eligible buyer wins the call.

      • Buyer match rules
      • Live bids and ping/post
      • Caps, hours and concurrency
      • Failover if a buyer misses

      85ms average routing time, from first ring to buyer.

      Publisher — Sends lead data
      PING — Check available buyers, get bids
      Buyer Matching — Hours, caps, geo, filters, duplicates
      POST — Get tracking number for the call
      Connected — Caller routes to selected buyer
